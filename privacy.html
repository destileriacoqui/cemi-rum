Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information Destilería Coquí, Inc. collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Destilería Coquí, Inc. is located in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico. This policy applies to this website and to orders, accounts, tours, and tastings handled through it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact & order details — name, email, phone, and the contents and totals of your orders.
  • Pickup & shipping details — pickup choices and, where shipping applies, a delivery address within Puerto Rico.
  • Age & identity verification — confirmation that you are of legal drinking age and, where required, identity-verification results processed by our payment and identity providers. We store the verification status and method, not your date of birth or ID images.
  • Account information — if you create an optional account, your saved profile details and order history.
  • Technical data — basic information your browser sends, and a local "age verified" flag stored in your browser so the age gate is not shown again.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, fulfill, and communicate about your orders, pickups, and tour bookings.
  • To verify legal drinking age and identity as required for alcohol sales.
  • To provide customer support and send transactional emails (confirmations and status updates).
  • To meet legal, tax, and licensing obligations in Puerto Rico.
  • To protect against fraud and misuse.

4. Service providers

We share information with vendors only as needed to operate the business, including:

  • Stripe — payment processing and identity verification. Payment card details are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Supabase — secure storage of accounts, orders, and bookings.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and confirmation emails.

These providers process data under their own terms and security practices. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to keep your cart, remember that you passed the age gate, and maintain secure sessions. We do not require advertising cookies to use the site.

6. Data retention

We keep order, tax, and booking records for as long as needed to fulfill orders and to comply with Puerto Rico tax and alcohol-licensing recordkeeping requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

7. Your choices

  • You can check out as a guest without creating an account.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by contacting us, subject to records we must retain by law.
  • You can unsubscribe from non-essential email at any time; transactional order emails may still be sent.

8. Children

This site and its products are intended only for adults of legal drinking age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the legal drinking age. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the most recent revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact orders@prsugar.com or use our contact page.
