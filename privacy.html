Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Destilería Coquí, Inc. collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Destilería Coquí, Inc. is located in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico. This policy applies to this website and to orders, accounts, tours, and tastings handled through it.

2. How and why we process information

We collect and use personal information only as reasonably necessary to operate our business, process transactions, comply with our legal, tax, and alcohol-licensing obligations in Puerto Rico, prevent fraud, and provide customer service. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information we collect

  • Contact & order details — name, email, phone, and the contents and totals of your orders.
  • Billing & payment status — billing details and the payment status of your orders. Full payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by Destilería Coquí.
  • Pickup & shipping details — pickup choices and, where shipping applies, a delivery address within Puerto Rico.
  • Age & identity verification — confirmation that you are of legal drinking age and, where required, identity-verification results. We generally store verification status and related transaction records, while sensitive identity documents are processed by our payment and identity-verification providers unless otherwise required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, chargeback, or compliance purposes.
  • Account information — if you create an optional account, your saved profile details and order history.
  • Technical data — this may include IP address, browser type, device and operating system information, referring pages, and access times, along with a local "age verified" flag stored in your browser so the age gate is not shown again.

4. How we use your information

  • To process, fulfill, and communicate about your orders, pickups, and tour bookings.
  • To verify legal drinking age and identity as required for alcohol sales.
  • To provide customer support and send transactional emails (confirmations and status updates).
  • To meet legal, tax, and licensing obligations in Puerto Rico.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud, handle chargebacks, and protect the security of the website.
  • To protect against fraud and misuse.

5. Service providers

We share information with vendors only as needed to operate the business, including:

  • Stripe — payment processing and identity verification. Payment card details are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Supabase — secure storage of accounts, orders, and bookings.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and confirmation emails.

These providers may process your information under their own privacy policies and data-processing terms. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. However, no website, payment system, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. International visitors

Our website is operated from Puerto Rico and is intended primarily for customers located in Puerto Rico. By using the site, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in Puerto Rico and the United States, which may have data-protection laws different from those in your location.

8. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to keep your cart, remember that you passed the age gate, and maintain secure sessions. We do not require advertising cookies to use the site.

9. Data retention

We keep order, tax, payment, booking, fraud-prevention, and compliance records for as long as needed to provide the services, operate the business, resolve disputes, process refunds or chargebacks, and comply with legal, tax, accounting, alcohol-licensing, and regulatory obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymize, or securely retain it only as required or permitted by law.

10. Your choices

  • You can check out as a guest without creating an account.
  • You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or closure of your account or personal information by contacting us, but some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, chargeback, alcohol-licensing, or regulatory reasons.
  • We currently send only transactional and operational emails related to orders, bookings, and customer service. If we introduce optional newsletters or promotions in the future, we will send them only to customers who choose to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

11. Children

This website, its products, and its alcohol-related experiences are intended for adults who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to review and remove the information, subject to records we may be required or permitted to retain by law.

12. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the most recent revision. For privacy questions, privacy requests, or account-data requests, contact us through our contact page.
