Coquí Plaza Mayagüez  ·  Puerto Rico
I

La Sala de Cata

Sit down with our full spirits lineup. From Ron Coquí™ Blanco to Pitorro® de Coco and Carjaker's overproof rum — our staff walks you through each expression. Available to all visitors during opening hours. Children under 18 are welcome; tasting is for adults only.

II

The Production Floor

See how Pitorro® and Ron Coquí™ are made from the inside. Walk through active distillation equipment, the bottling line, and the quality control station. Our team is on hand to answer every question. This is the real thing.

III

The Distillery Shop

The best selection of Destilería Coquí products on the island — including distillery-exclusive bottlings, branded merchandise, and gift sets not available anywhere else. Open during all visiting hours. Free parking on site.
