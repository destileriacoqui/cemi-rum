Coquí Plaza.

A place to gather, taste, and make memories in Mayagüez. Visit us for guided experiences, special events, and a closer look at the world of Coquí.

Explore

La Plaza

Come for a taste.
Stay for the moment.

Coquí Plaza is our home for visitors. Join a guided tasting, discover the bottles made in Mayagüez, and watch for special gatherings throughout the year.

I

Guided Tastings

Discover the flavors and stories behind our spirits with a guided tasting. Children under 18 are welcome with a paying adult; tasting is for adults only.

II

Events & Gatherings

Coquí Plaza brings people together for tastings, celebrations, and special moments. Follow our events calendar for upcoming gatherings in Mayagüez.

III

The Distillery Shop

The best selection of Destilería Coquí, Inc. products on the island — including distillery-exclusive bottlings, branded merchandise, and gift sets not available anywhere else. Open during all visiting hours. Free parking on site.
