Shipping & Returns

How pickup, shipping, identification, and refunds work for orders from Destilería Coquí, Inc.

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Where we ship

Where legally permitted and available, we ship only within Puerto Rico. We do not ship alcoholic beverages outside of Puerto Rico. Orders with a delivery address outside Puerto Rico cannot be fulfilled and will be canceled and refunded.

Shipping or delivery may be unavailable for certain products, addresses, locations, carriers, license restrictions, verification issues, or operational reasons, even within Puerto Rico.

2. Identification at delivery & pickup

  • Every alcohol order requires a recipient of legal drinking age in Puerto Rico (18 years of age or older) with valid government-issued photo ID.
  • We require photo identification from any person who appears to be younger than 27 before selling, releasing, delivering, or allowing pickup of alcoholic beverages.
  • Alcohol will not be released or delivered without valid ID.
  • The name on the order should match the person receiving it.
  • Orders may be refused, returned, or refunded if ID cannot be verified.

3. Shipping fees & taxes

Where shipping is offered, a flat shipping fee of $20 is added once per shipped order, regardless of the number of bottles, unless a different fee is shown at checkout. Puerto Rico Sales and Use Tax (IVU) applies to taxable products and to shipping, delivery, handling, service, convenience, or similar fees when required by applicable Puerto Rico law. All applicable taxes and fees will be shown before checkout.

4. Processing & timing

Orders are prepared from our location in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico. Pickup and shipping timing depends on availability and, for Express Pickup, on the option selected at checkout. We will contact you by email or text when a pickup order is ready.

Processing times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Weather, holidays, inventory, verification delays, carrier issues, or other circumstances may affect pickup or delivery timing.

5. Pickup

Pickup orders carry no shipping fee. You may pay online in advance or pay in person at pickup where that option is offered. Express Pickup, where available, carries a separate fee and targets a faster pickup window subject to product availability, operating hours, and verification requirements. Any applicable taxes or fees will be shown before checkout.

6. Returns & refunds

All alcohol sales are final once released or delivered, except for damaged, incorrect, missing, or legally required returns or refunds. Because these are alcoholic beverages, we generally cannot accept returns of opened products, and some products may not be returnable once released, except where required by law. We may issue a refund or replacement when:

  • An order is canceled before fulfillment.
  • An item arrives damaged, missing, or incorrect.
  • We are unable to verify age or identity and the order is refused.
  • A delivery cannot be completed because no qualifying adult is available — a refund may be reduced by non-recoverable shipping or handling costs.

Damage, missing item, or incorrect item claims should be reported within 48 hours of delivery or pickup, with photos where applicable. We may require photos, packaging, bottle condition, order details, and other reasonable evidence before approving a refund or replacement.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe or the original payment provider. Refund timing may depend on the payment provider and the customer's bank.

7. Order problems & contact

If something is wrong with your order, contact us as soon as possible through our contact page so we can make it right.
