Pitorro®, made legal.

Héctor Augusto and María Cristina founded Destilería Coquí, Inc. in 2006 with a clear purpose: honor Puerto Rico’s centuries-old Pitorro® tradition and give it a legal home in Mayagüez.

Before Coquí, Pitorro® lived in the shadows: homemade, unlabeled, and technically illegal. They fought to formalize the island’s Ron Caña, trademarked Pitorro®, and brought the island’s most beloved spirit into the light.

“Pitorro® was always made here, in every barrio, in every family’s backyard. We simply refused to let that tradition disappear.”
